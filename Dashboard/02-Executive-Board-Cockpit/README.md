# Executive Board Cockpit

## Objective

Provide the Board and CFO with a one-page summary of business performance.

### Key KPIs

- Revenue Growth
- AUM Growth
- PAT Growth
- ROA
- ROE
- Net Interest Margin
- Cost-to-Income Ratio
- Credit Cost
- GNPA
- Capital Adequacy Ratio

### Business Questions Answered

- Are we growing profitably?
- Are operating costs under control?
- Is credit quality improving?
- Do we have sufficient capital?
- Which KPIs require management attention?

### Management Action

This dashboard is reviewed monthly by the CFO and Executive Leadership Team.
